ARGUS

Live data from free public sources — no accounts, no keys, no backend, nothing invented. Unreachable sources say so.

HOW TO USE

  • Map — all layers always on: quakes, micro-quakes, storms, airports, air quality, night shade, ISS + track. Fixed single-world view (no zoom/pan) — click any marker for detail. Feeds auto-refresh.
  • Click the map — instant place name, country facts, weather in the strip below.
  • News dropdown — 9 world regions, live.
  • Status bar — live state of every source; anything unreachable says so.
